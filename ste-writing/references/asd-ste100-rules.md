# ASD-STE100：具體規則摘要

來源：[官方 Issue 9，2025-01-15](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf)。查核日期：2026-10-26。

以下以英文濃縮九章的重點，括號為官方規則編號。這是選錄摘要，不是完整規範、逐條翻譯或受控詞典；未列出的細節仍以原文為準。

1. **Vocabulary (1.1–1.4, 1.7–1.14):** Use dictionary-approved words or technical nouns/verbs. Respect meanings, roles, and forms. Keep technical names consistent. Default to US spelling unless directed otherwise.
2. **Noun groups (2.1–2.2):** Limit groups to three words; introduce longer technical names before shortening or clarifying their grouping.
3. **Verbs (3.1–3.7):** Use infinitives, commands, simple present/past/future, and adjectival past participles. Restrict “-ing” to technical nouns/modifiers. Use active voice; passive description requires an unknown agent.
4. **Sentences (4.1–4.5):** Write clearly without grammatical omissions or contractions. Organize complexity into lists; retain connectors and necessary determiners.
5. **Procedures (5.1–5.5):** Maximum 20 words per sentence. Give one command unless actions coincide. Put prerequisite conditions first. Notes supply information, not commands.
6. **Descriptions (6.1–6.6):** Develop information progressively. Maximum 25 words per sentence; six sentences per paragraph. Give each paragraph one topic.
7. **Safety (7.1–7.3):** Identify severity. Begin with the relevant command/condition; explain the hazard or consequence.
8. **Punctuation (8.1–8.7):** Exclude semicolons. Hyphens clarify grouping. Parenthesized content, identifiers, and hyphenated compounds count individually as single words. List-introducing colons end sentences for counting.
9. **Revision (9.1–9.4):** Restructure beyond substitution; preserve approved meanings. Avoid phrasal verbs. Maintain consistent wording.

用於一般文章、對話或非英文內容時，依主檔的跨語言與文體調整處理。核對官方規則時，請使用此處所列 Issue 9 的編號；不同版本的編號與內容可能不同。
